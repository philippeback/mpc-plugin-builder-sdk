8 Performance Macros (2 Q-Link Banks)

Perform full-chain signal corruption from a single screen of interactive 180×180 orb-knobs. Every macro directly updates and reflects the controls on the detail tabs so you can switch pages and fine-tune any parameter naturally; at 0%, macros leave all base parameters untouched.

Bank 1 — Core Destruction & Choke

EXHAUSTION

Engages S-Curve, scales input drive (+12x), and pushes saturation toward hard clipping with automatic headroom trim.

CHOKE

Sweeps Filter A downward and Filter B inward (or both downward together in Parallel mode) while boosting resonance for instant acid/industrial build-ups.

INFO DECAY

Drops bit depth toward 2-bit and ramps sample-rate downsampling up to 12x while illuminating the pixelated S-CURVE decay bay.

CORRUPT MIX

Drives global wet mix toward 100% while injecting the internal Noise Oscillator into the filter stage.

Bank 2 — Motion, Space & Rhythm

KINETIC MOD

Opens LFO1 & LFO2 filter modulation depths while accelerating both LFOs from slow drift to rapid wobble.

RESONATOR

Engages short metallic comb-delay feedback toward self-oscillation alongside Filter A/B grit.

GATE CARVE

Morphs the tempo-synced Trance Gate from subtle pulsing into razor-sharp 1/16th rhythmic chops with synced Floor depth.

SMASH

Engages the final Limiter in LMC squash mode with up to +18 dB of drive for instant drum-bus punch.

Standalone Deployment

Installing on Your MPC

Compatible with MPC X, X SE, Live, Live II, One, One+, Key 61, Key 37, Force (32-bit) and MPC Live III / XL (64-bit).

  1. Download the latest Mushin release zip from GitHub Releases and unzip it (or build from plugins/Mushin with python tools/build.py plugins/Mushin).
  2. Ensure your hardware runs MPC OS 3.10.1 or later.
  3. On the MPC, open Preferences → Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support.
  4. Copy the folder PhilippeBack - VST - Mushin into the VST2 Plugins folder on your MPC’s USB drive or SD card (or deploy over SSH via tools/mpc_remote.py).
  5. Load Mushin from the MPC browser under External Plugins on any audio or instrument insert.
