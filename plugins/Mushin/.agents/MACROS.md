For *Mushin* on the standalone MPC, a dedicated **`MAIN` (Performance) lead tab** locked to **8 macros (2 Q-Link banks of 4)** lets you perform full-chain signal corruption without tab-diving across `S-CURVE`, `FILTER`, `LFO`, `LFO MTX`, `GATE`, `DLY`, and `LIM` during a beat.

### Bank 1 — Core Destruction & Choke (Row 1, Q1–Q4)

1. **EXHAUSTION (Drive & S-Curve Bias):** Scales input drive (`+12x`) and pushes S-Curve soft saturation toward odd-order hard clipping, while gently compensating output trim so the signal gets gnarlier without blowing up insert headroom.
2. **CHOKE (Dual Filter Sweep):** Sweeps Filter A cutoff downward and Filter B cutoff inward while scaling resonance up (`+0.35`)—instant acid/industrial build-up on a single Q-Link turn.
3. **INFO DECAY (Quantize & Sample-Rate Crush):** Blends bit-depth reduction down toward 2-bit and increases downsampling up to `12x` for crisp digital crunch.
4. **CORRUPT MIX (Parallel Wet + Noise Injection):** Sweeps global wet mix toward `100%` while injecting the internal Noise Oscillator into the filter stage—ideal for snare/synth risers.

### Bank 2 — Motion, Space & Rhythm (Row 3, Q1–Q4)

5. **KINETIC MOD (LFO1 + LFO2 Depth & Rate):** Opens `LFO1 -> A Cut` and `LFO2 -> B Cut/Res` modulation depths while accelerating LFO rates from slow drift into rapid wobble.
6. **RESONATOR (Comb / Delay Feedback & Grit):** Engages short metallic delay feedback toward self-oscillation alongside Filter A/B Grit, turning percussive hits into pitched metallic drones.
7. **GATE CARVE (Trance Gate Depth & Hold):** Morphs the tempo-synced Trance Gate from subtle pulsing to razor-sharp 1/16th chops by tightening gate hold and raising gate depth.
8. **SMASH (Limiter Drive & LMC Squash):** Engages the final Limiter in LMC squash mode, driving input up to `+18 dB` against the ceiling for immediate drum-bus punch and glue.
