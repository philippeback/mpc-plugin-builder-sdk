For *Mushin* on the standalone MPC, a dedicated **`MAIN` (Performance) tab** locked to **8 macros (2 Q-Link banks of 4)** lets you perform full-chain signal corruption without ever tab-diving across `S-CURVE`, `FILTER`, `LFO MOD`, `MOD 2`, and `SIDECHN` during a beat.

### Bank 1 — Core Destruction & Choke (Q1–Q4)

1. **EXHAUSTION (Drive & S-Curve Bias):** Scales input gain and S-Curve soft-saturation toward odd-order hard clipping, while gently compensating output trim so the signal gets gnarlier without blowing up the insert headroom.
2. **CHOKE (Dual Filter Sweep):** Moves Filter A cutoff downward and Filter B cutoff inward (band-choking the midrange) while scaling resonance up slightly—instant acid/industrial build-up on a single Q-Link turn.
3. **INFO DECAY (Quantize & Sample-Rate Crush):** Blends bit-depth reduction down toward 2-bit with subtle high-shelf compensation so digital crunch cuts through the mix instead of turning muddy.
4. **CORRUPT MIX (Parallel Dry/Wet + Noise Injection):** Sweeps global wet mix from 0% to 100% while nudging the internal Noise Level into the filter stage—ideal for snare/synth risers.



### Bank 2 — Motion, Space & Rhythm (Q5–Q8)

5. **KINETIC MOD (LFO1 + LFO2 Depth & Rate):** Simultaneously opens `LFO1` and `LFO2->B Cut/Res` modulation depths and accelerates LFO rate from slow drift into audio-rate FM wobble.


6. **RESONATOR (Comb / Delay Feedback & Grit):** Pushes short delay feedback toward self-oscillation alongside Filter A/B Grit, turning percussive hits into pitched metallic drones.


7. **GATE CARVE (Trance Gate Depth & Decay):** Morphs the tempo-synced Trance Gate from subtle rhythmic pulsing to razor-sharp 1/16th chops by tightening gate decay and raising gate depth.
8. **PUMP / DUCK (Sidechain Depth):** Scales sidechain envelope routing onto both drive and output amplitude so a bassline or pad immediately breathes around the kick.
